About Me

I'm a transfem student studying Computer Science and Linguistics at The University of Chicago. I was born and raised in Portland, Oregon. I'm also a vegetarian NixOS user.

I'm primarily interested in compilers and programming languages, both formal and natural. however, I'm also fascinated by systems programming generally. as a transfem, my language of choice is of course Rust (although C is a close second).

some of my favorite media:

  • Doctor Who, a long-running British Sci-Fi show about the titular Doctor Who (no, it's not "The Doctor"; real fans get it)
  • Modem Prometheus, an urban fantasy podcast by namtao
  • Patricia Taxxon, a Hyperpop and experimental electronic musician, she's also trans and makes great video essays

beyond my laptop screen, I enjoy poetry, biking, urban environments, typewriters, and tea.
