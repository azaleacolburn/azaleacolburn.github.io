By Azalea Colburn

a small surrender

watch
this acorn drop,

it cracks, splintered,
against the august pavement
burnt black by the harsh
southern sun, summers since
past until cracked and
hardened oil paved the path
where older acorns
fell and grew.

but the crow stares down,
its dark eyes indifferent
as an engine roars below
and the acorn
smashs to a pulp,
laid bare,
exposed to the crow
which its ancestors
long ago conquered.
