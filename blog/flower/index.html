By Azalea Colburn

flower of the word

the fragile flower
—three days dead,
wilted red—
blooms through

the forest floor
of tight knit grass
—the narrow path—

pushing dew drops down,
back to the ground,
to the soil,

as pollen sprays
and spreads on winds
out to the world
—the word.

the flower is born once more
in the forest grey and brown
—which tore itself apart
in search of color—
next spring
