By Azalea Colburn

vertical integration

atop mounds of sand,
pillars of smoke and iron
stand
lattices of falling leaves

where little metal men slide up and down
along the grooves and grain, the sea
of fog and soot and wood
and piercing—

hexagons, basalt
from scorched,
assaulted earth

and all across them
all along arrays
of pipes and pumps
there lay lines on lay lines

witches burn and trees rot:
silver and stone
to carbon and steel,

clockwork alive and
infinitely high I—

the ground is gone now
and i cannot see the sky
nor fly

so i climb
ducts down
polls up ladders and

try not to fall, just
a little longer
