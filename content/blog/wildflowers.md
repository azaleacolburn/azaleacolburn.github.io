i have seen the best minds of my generation cowering in coffeehouses,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;cold brew burnt out of this world and pulled back through!  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;rot consumed the wooden planks and knots that bind them are to blame?!  
ecstasy! broken typists lay around us, endangered yet blindingly aware,  
who trade ginger and sell roots while urban structures still stand  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;purchase focus and forgetfulness,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;abstract freedom found in screens,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;owned by corpses of collective action and corporate board meetings  

whose plugged in spirits burst and bubble with memory mappings and pointers to a void,  
who work faster now&mdash;        
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;quiet screaming, number than before  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;harmonizing with the other strung up souls  
who revel in mechanical sanctity, divested of their own,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;dogs for the towers which shrewdly shelter them,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;laying bricks for the great mechanical edifice:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a bridge to cross the rubicon.  

who quench their fears with homegrown needles, hoping to hit arteries.  
who forget secrets each day, meet again with each blackened sip and retold open lie.  
who cling to shreds of liberalism, once discarded for red-black wet dreams  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;raise up glasses by the ocean, watch meek waves lap  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;against the cliffs of Oregon Country as the Great Lakes iced blue  
who hide in the museum&mdash;words! words! at 22:50 AM&mdash;among the art called  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;dolls to cynically hide the pain&mdash;yet in its promises and doubts art _will_ suffice!  

whose paranoid fits leave them backwards on chairs, facing broken cameras and torn up curtains.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;which once they called their only home, now screaming hysterically naked  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for no one or the world to see  
who worship under the blinding, burning light, smoldering in chiaroscuro.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;godspurned screams to up to the sky  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;wander one more forty years before He'll hear you  
who chew on bitter chalk, dry up, cease skinning thoughts, voice ripping fantasies.  
who push it down their throats  
    relish in the salt, cry at diltation and collapse  

who crave that queer, blessed unrest  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that humming, buzzing air that keeps them marching  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and makes them more alive than the others  

who  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;agony and terror forced them through splintering wooden doors, into the cafe lights  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;feel curse marks acrossed their necks&mdash;branded by doctors, lovers, and false gods  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;yearn for her unclean, kosher body&mdash;born broken, piercing, demonettes&mdash;and can't bear it!  

who find themselves as failed men, deluded and reshaped.  
who lean closer, weakening at the sight of her spindled hands  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;grazing their tattooed freckles&mdash;basilisk bug&mdash;to be crumbled by its emerging eyespots  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;as whispered ballads, two-part soliloquies of ecstasy through hours lived and slept  

who walk onto grass, away from the light  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;find the deepest darkness between buildings  
who arch their backs, held yet closer through,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;shrieking and moaning  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;pierced by silver and peachwood  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;squeezing chitin and porcelain  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;rend the flesh from their skin  

who twirl and pretend, gaze longingly, skating down cobbled streets  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;to find themselves at train stops,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;with dreams of wildflowers and rusted boats
