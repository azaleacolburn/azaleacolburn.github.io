# Ambiguity Machines: A Review

A few years back, on a month long trip through Finland and the Baltic states, I read the science fiction collection: *Ambiguity Machines And Other Stories*, by author and physicist Vandana Singh and it has been stewing in my mind ever since. Importantly, for this trip I didn't have my phone, instead getting entertainment from this book and a blank journal, which I filled as I made my way south (additionally, my orienteering skills greatly strengthened).
