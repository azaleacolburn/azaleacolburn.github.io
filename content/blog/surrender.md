watch&mdash;  
the acorn drops,  

it cracks, splintered  
against the august pavement  
burnt black by the harsh  
southern sun, summers past  
since spalled and  
hardened oil spilled  
across the land  
where ancient acorns  
fell and grew.  

but the crow stares down,  
its dark eyes indifferent  
as engines roar below  
and the acorn  
is smashed to a pulp,  
laid bare,  
exposed to the crow,  
which its ancestors  
long ago conquered
