watch  
the acorn drop,  

it cracks, splintered  
against the august pavement  
burnt black by the harsh  
southern sun, summers past  
since spalled and  
hardened oil spilled  
across the land  
where older acorns  
fell and grew.  

but the crow stares down,  
its dark eyes indifferent  
as an engine roars below  
and the acorn  
smashs to a pulp,  
laid bare,  
exposed to the crow,  
which its ancestors  
long ago conquered
